# Mercury Auto — Privacy Policy
Effective date: 12 November 2026

Mercury Auto is an independent OBD companion. It does not require an account and contains no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. The application does not send diagnostic readings, VIN, vehicle profiles or saved reports to a developer server.

## Vehicle data and permissions
The app communicates with the OBD adapter you select over your local Wi-Fi network or Bluetooth LE. It requests local-network and Bluetooth permissions for that purpose. Vehicle readings, supported VIN responses and diagnostic trouble codes are processed on the device. The app does not use location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

## Local storage and sharing
Vehicle profile and adapter preferences are stored locally. Diagnostic reports are stored only when you choose to save them. iOS backup may include local app data. You can delete all saved reports from the Reports screen; uninstalling the app removes its local container, subject to backup retention controlled by Apple.

Report sharing is optional and uses the iOS share sheet. VIN is omitted by default unless you enable its inclusion. The destination you choose then handles the shared information under its own policy. Do not share a full VIN or other personal information in a public support issue.

## External services
Links to BMW information open external websites whose policies apply to those visits. GitHub hosts this support documentation and the support issue tracker; it is not an in-app analytics or diagnostic collection service.

## Support and changes
For privacy questions or support, open an issue at https://github.com/valentinnedelcu1974-arch/iEnergy-iData-iOS/issues with the title “Mercury Auto privacy” and omit vehicle identifiers or sensitive data. This policy will be updated if the application's practices change.
